Elastic Admin — your admin, handled

Elastic Admin runs the books for trade businesses. Connect the Gmail inboxes where your receipts and bills land, and Ava — the Elastic Admin assistant — reads them to find the paperwork, matches each document to the right bank transaction in Xero, and preps your books. With your permission she can also send from your inbox — only to chase a supplier for a missing receipt or reply where you've asked her to; she never deletes or modifies mail. The Elastic team checks and reconciles everything. You can disconnect an inbox at any time.
